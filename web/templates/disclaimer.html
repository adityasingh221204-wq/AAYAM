What AAYAM Is NOT For

To ensure responsible deployment and protect Indian citizens from misplaced reliance, this document sets forth the explicit legal, technical, and operational boundaries of the AAYAM decision-support system.

⚠️

Important Declaration for All Users

AAYAM is an educational, research-backed decision-support system. It does not replace registered financial planners, commercial antivirus solutions, or official law enforcement authorities. In an ongoing financial fraud emergency, your first action must always be dialing 1930.

❌ What AAYAM Is EXPLICITLY NOT

🚫
NOT SEBI Financial Advice
  • AAYAM does NOT provide individualized stock picks, equity recommendations, crypto trading advice, or mutual fund portfolio management.
  • All resilience calculations (DTI, runway months) are mathematical benchmarks calibrated to median decile cash flows, not personalized investment guidance.
  • Consult a registered SEBI Investment Adviser (RIA) before committing household capital to financial securities.
🛡️
NOT An Antivirus / Firewall
  • CyberDrishti is NOT an endpoint detection and response (EDR) agent, resident antivirus, or real-time network interception firewall.
  • CyberDrishti screens text, links, and manifests heuristically. A 'Safe' verdict does not guarantee that an external binary is free of zero-day exploits.
  • Do not disable Google Play Protect, device firewalls, or standard smartphone security controls.
🚓
NOT A Law Enforcement Portal
  • AAYAM is NOT an official police station, judicial body, or direct First Information Report (FIR) lodging system.
  • Entering incident text into AAYAM does NOT notify the police or trigger a legal investigation on your behalf.
  • To file a formal police complaint, you must dial 1930 or register at cybercrime.gov.in.
🏦
NOT A Loan Broker Or Disburser
  • AAYAM does NOT sanction loans, collect application processing fees, or guarantee bank credit approvals.
  • The Opportunity Pathways directory provides educational matching to official government schemes (e.g. Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana, PMKVY).
  • Actual loan disbursement is subject solely to underwriting criteria enforced by lending banks and financial institutions.

✅ What AAYAM IS Specifically Designed For

💡
Educational Decision Support
  • Empowers households to diagnose cash flow vulnerabilities, model emergency runway months, and eliminate discretionary debt drag.
  • Pre-tests resilience against sudden job loss or medical crises using transparent, deterministic forward simulations.
🔎
Pre-Transaction Heuristic Screening
  • Enables citizens to screen suspicious SMS, fake electricity disconnection alerts, reverse UPI QR codes, and digital arrest threats.
  • Breaks down deceptive psychological patterns into explainable threat indicators before money leaves the bank.
⏱️
Golden Hour Crisis Guidance
  • Provides victims of financial fraud with immediate, step-by-step checklists to contact bank toll-free fraud desks.
  • Directs victims to the National Cyber Crime Helpline (1930) within the critical 2-hour window.
🏛️
Public Provenance Transparency
  • All national economic metrics and cyber statistics are grounded in published reports from RBI, NPCI, MoSPI, and MHA registers.
  • Completely auditable research references with zero synthetic hallucinated citations.
DATA ETHICS & PRIVACY

Zero Credential Retention Pledge

🔒

AAYAM is engineered with a strict Privacy-by-Design architecture:

  • No Credential Ingestion: We never prompt for, store, or transmit bank account numbers, debit/credit card CVVs, net-banking passwords, UPI PINs, or Aadhaar numbers.
  • In-Memory Processing: Threat screening text and financial twin parameters are evaluated ephemerally in-memory or on the client browser. No communication text is logged into external marketing databases.
  • No Tracking Cookies: AAYAM does not utilize third-party commercial advertising trackers or sell citizen telemetry to private insurance firms or lending aggregators.
Official Reporting Notice: If you suspect an active ongoing crime or terror threat, report it directly to your nearest state police station or through the official MHA portal at https://cybercrime.gov.in.
