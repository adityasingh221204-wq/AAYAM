🎯 Problem Formulation & Societal Significance:

Formalized analysis of household economic fragility in India's informal workforce contrasted against the rapid surge of digital extortion.

🔄 Phase-I to Phase-II System Evolution

Phase-I Baseline: Focused solely on static household budget tabulation and basic savings percentages without dynamic risk modeling, cyber threat defense, or multi-dataset macro alignment.

Phase-II Multi-Dimensional System (AAYAM): Refined into a dual-engine decision-support platform bridging economic mobility with citizen cyber-financial defense:

  • Integrated MoSPI PLFS (wages), HCES (expenditure), ASUSE (MSME productivity), and RBI Payment Data.
  • Engineered the Economic Resilience Index (ERI) with runway simulation under inflation and job-loss shocks.
  • Created CyberDrishti: Real-time, explainable screener for digital arrest, reverse UPI QR, and APK scams.
  • Implemented 1930 Golden Hour Incident Response to prevent capital depletion.

👥 Key Stakeholders & Beneficiaries in India

1. Gig & Informal Wage Earners: Delivery partners, drivers, and support staff living with under 1.5 months emergency runway.
2. First-Generation Digital Banking Citizens: Rural and semi-urban migrants targeted by WhatsApp/SMS extortion and malicious APKs.
3. Micro-Entrepreneurs (Mudra Beneficiaries): Nano-businesses seeking Shishu/Kishore financing to scale enterprise productivity.
4. Vulnerable Consumers: Elderly citizens coerced by fabricated "Digital Arrest" warrants or disconnection penalties.

🇮🇳 Institutional Alignment & Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)

SDG 1 — No Poverty (Target 1.5): Builds resilience of vulnerable households against income shocks and digital fraud losses.
SDG 8 — Decent Work & Economic Growth: Connects informal workers to formal certifications and Mudra credit pathways.
MHA I4C & NCRP 1930: Supports National Cybercrime Reporting Portal guidelines to freeze stolen capital within the 2-hour window.

🎯 Algorithmic Architecture & Mathematical Formulation:

Complete ML pipeline combining tree-based ensemble learning, deterministic rule-augmented NLP, and discrete-event Monte Carlo shock simulation.

🧮 Mathematical Formulation: Economic Resilience Index (ERI)

The composite score \( \text{ERI} \in [0, 100] \) is formulated as a multi-criteria utility function balancing immediate solvency against systemic fragility:

ERI = 0.35 • S_runway + 0.25 • S_surplus + 0.25 • S_debt + 0.15 • S_stability

Where \( S_{\text{runway}} = \min(35, \frac{\text{Savings}}{\text{Expenses}} \cdot \frac{35}{6.0}) \), \( S_{\text{surplus}} = \min(25, \frac{\text{Surplus}}{\text{Income}} \cdot 50) \), and \( S_{\text{debt}} \) penalizes DTI exceeding 40%.

⚖️ Algorithm Selection & Theoretical Justification

Task / Engine Chosen Algorithm Alternatives Tested Superiority Justification
Resilience Classification Random Forest & GBDT Deep MLP, SVM Tabular data contains non-linear interactions between income and debt. Tree ensembles are immune to feature scale distortions, require zero gradient tuning, and provide exact feature importances.
Cyber Threat Screener Rule-Augmented NLP Pure LLM, Naive Bayes Zero false-negative tolerance required for extortion. LLMs hallucinate and introduce 1.5s+ latency; our weighted signature engine executes in <1.8ms with forensic explainability.
Shock Trajectory (ArthSim) Discrete-Event Simulation ARIMA, Linear Regression ARIMA cannot model discrete external policy or catastrophic shocks (e.g. 100% job loss or ₹50k fraud loss). Discrete simulation calculates exact month-by-month depletion curves.
