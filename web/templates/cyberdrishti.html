Top 5 Active Fraud Vectors Detected in India (2025)

CRITICAL

Digital Arrest & Video Extortion

Impersonating CBI or Mumbai Customs on Skype video with fake arrest warrants. Demands asset liquidation into 'security accounts'.

CRITICAL

Reverse UPI QR Trap

Fraudster sends a QR code claiming it will 'credit' funds or marketplace sale. Entering UPI PIN debits the victim's account instantly.

HIGH

Utility Disconnection Notice

Urgent SMS threatening same-night power or gas cut. Prompts victim to call a personal phone number or sideload a rogue APK.

HIGH

Remote Access Tool (RAT) Prompts

Tricking users into installing AnyDesk, RustDesk, or TeamViewer under the guise of customer support to read bank OTPs.
