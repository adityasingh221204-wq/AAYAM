Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: Does AAYAM store my bank account details, UPI PIN, or passwords?

Absolute Zero Retention: AAYAM never requests or stores bank account numbers, passwords, UPI PINs, Aadhaar numbers, or OTPs. All financial calculations run in-memory on your browser or ephemeral server without logging personal financial identifiers.

Q2: What is the Golden Hour in cyber fraud?

The Golden Hour refers to the first 2 hours following an unauthorized financial transaction. During this window, dialing 1930 (National Cybercrime Helpline) allows the Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C) to trigger automated holds on beneficiary mule accounts across 250+ banks before funds can be withdrawn at ATMs.

Q3: How does CyberDrishti detect scams without an antivirus?

CyberDrishti is an explainable heuristic text and payload analyzer. It evaluates four deterministic pillars: (1) Urgency & Fear, (2) Financial Trigger, (3) Authority Impersonation, and (4) Technical Payload signatures. It matches communications against verified CERT-In and MHA attack patterns.

Q4: What should AAYAM NOT be used for?

AAYAM is an educational decision-support copilot. It is NOT SEBI-registered stock advice, NOT an antivirus software, and NOT an official police station for filing FIRs. Read our full Ethical Guardrails & Warning Page for explicit boundaries.
