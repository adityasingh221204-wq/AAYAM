Economic Digital Twin

Your personal household financial diagnostic engine. Model cash flows, isolate discretionary expenditure leaks, measure your debt-to-income burden, and compute your composite Economic Resilience Score (0–100) with deterministic precision.

📖 How To Use The Digital Twin
4 SIMPLE STEPS
1
Select a Persona or Input

Pick a preset benchmark (Salaried, Gig Worker, Merchant) or enter your actual monthly income & savings.

2
Separate Expenses

Distinguish between essential survival expenses (rent, food, utilities) and discretionary lifestyle drag.

3
Observe Resilience

Watch your Emergency Runway (months) and Resilience Score update dynamically in real time.

Stress-Test in ArthSim

Export your twin profile or proceed to ArthSim to stress-test your balance against economic shocks.
