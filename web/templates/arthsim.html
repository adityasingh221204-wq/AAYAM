ArthSim Scenario Simulator

Stress-test your household or micro-enterprise finances against abrupt economic shocks before they happen in reality. Measure the exact month your liquid savings would deplete during sudden job loss, cyber theft, medical crises, or inflation spikes.

📖 How To Run A Shock Stress-Test
4 SIMPLE STEPS
1
Select Shock Scenario

Choose from Job Loss, Cyber Fraud Loss, Critical Medical Emergency, Inflation Surge, or Dual Shock.

2
Set Projection Horizon

Pick a forward observation window of 6, 12, 18, or 24 months to model the multi-stage recovery arc.

3
Simulate Trajectory

Click "Run Shock Simulation" to compute monthly savings balance points and visualize the depletion curve.

4
Read Contingency Guidance

Review the calculated depletion month and adopt recommended discretionary cuts and liquidity safeguards.
