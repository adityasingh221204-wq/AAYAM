Our 4-Step Citizen Resilience Journey

Follow this simple, step-by-step roadmap to assess your financial baseline, unlock new earnings, and protect your digital savings.

01 A-01

Personal Financial Twin & Diagnosis

Input your monthly income, living expenses, and debts to compute your Resilience Score (0–100) and Emergency Runway months.

What happens: AAYAM calculates whether your monthly budget has a cash surplus or operating deficit. It reveals how many months your savings will survive if you suddenly lose your job or suffer an emergency.
+
02 B-02

Opportunity Matching & Mudra Linkages

AI ranks verified wage jobs and micro-enterprises tailored to your skills, with government PMKVY 4.0 certifications and Mudra loan support.

What happens: AAYAM evaluates 4 transparent factors: your skill overlap, qualification alignment, capital affordability, and potential income increase (e.g. +₹15,000 to ₹35,000/mo).
+
03 C-03

Real-Time Cyber Scam & Phishing Screening

Received a suspicious message or call? Paste it into CyberDrishti to test against 13+ attack patterns before you click or transfer money.

What happens: Checks for fake police/CBI digital arrests, deceptive reverse UPI QR codes, fraudulent APK downloads, electricity bill traps, and KYC expiry lures.
+
04 D-04

1930 Golden Hour Emergency Recovery

If unauthorized money was deducted, follow the time-critical 2-hour playbook to freeze stolen funds and file a formal zero-liability dispute.

What happens: Step-by-step guidance on reporting to the 1930 National Helpline, blocking your debit card/UPI handle, and asserting your statutory rights under RBI's 72-Hour Zero Liability Circular.

🆘 Recovery Centre — Incident Response Playbook

⏳ Golden 2-Hour Window Notice: If you suffered an unauthorized bank transfer, immediately call 1930 or report to cybercrime.gov.in so law enforcement can put a lien on the beneficiary account before funds are laundered.

Immediate 3-Step Incident Protocol:

1

Report on 1930 National Helpline

Call 1930 or visit cybercrime.gov.in. Keep your transaction reference number (UTR/RRN), debit bank account, and date/time ready.

2

Contact Bank to Block Channels

Call your bank's 24x7 fraud desk immediately to block your debit card, UPI handle, and netbanking password.

SBI: 1800 11 1109 • HDFC: 1800 1600 • ICICI: 1800 1080 • Axis: 1800 419 5959
3

Invoke RBI 72-Hour Zero Liability

Under RBI Circular DBR.No.Leg.BC.78/09.07.005/2017-18, if an unauthorized transaction occurs through third-party breach and you notify your bank within 3 working days, you have Zero Customer Liability.
